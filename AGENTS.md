Read ../end_of_abyss/STATUS.md and ../../template/launch_gamesites.md current stage before work. Sources: ../end_of_abyss/_research/facts.md and pain-points.md. Never invent coordinates, DPS, boss phase thresholds, upgrade costs or a Steam release.

## Design Context
Audience: English players checking a blocked route on a phone while playing. The useful outcome is a specific next action, with resource cost and prerequisites clear. Visual language comes from the game's black-blue facility art, with readable pale prose and a deliberate narrow display heading. No eyebrow labels, metric-strip hero or repeated generic icon cards. Respect keyboard navigation and reduced motion.
